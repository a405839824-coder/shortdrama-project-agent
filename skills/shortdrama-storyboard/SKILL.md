## Enforce the 0.5.5 generation-first quality gate

For every new full storyboard or complete scene/episode rebuild in plugin mode, execute this exact order without skipping or reordering:

1. Call `get_agent_status(project_id)`. If it reports `migration_required`, stop ordinary production and use an authorized rule-improvement/version-management work order to call `migrate_project_state(action="plan")`, inspect the before/after hashes and compatibility report, then call `migrate_project_state(action="execute")`. A legacy-event migration must seal them in a replay checkpoint bound to the explicit signed-journal migration ID, old-ledger canonical hash, prefix hashes, scope aliases, and round boundaries; every later load/mutation must verify that checkpoint against the signed migration journal and CAS. The checkpoint's requirement marker is not itself authorization. Do not continue until status is valid and executable.
2. Call `query_project_issues` before generation and bind the returned active prevention profile to the production task. Recurrence identity is pattern-based across batches and versions; an episode, filename, shot ID, or mutable evidence string is an occurrence location, not part of the defect identity.
3. Generate one canonical semantic truth and call `preflight_storyboard_draft(scope_kind="scene")` after every complete scene. A failed scene preflight must be repaired and rerun before generating or accepting downstream scenes. The service records the failed draft event and updates recurrence/prevention evidence, but creates no artifact, version, XLSX, export projection, successor pointer, or handoff.
4. After every scene is clean, call `preflight_storyboard_draft(scope_kind="full")` on the exact final canonical. Preserve its signed `draft_preflight_id`; a different canonical, rule, validator, session, work order, prevention-profile hash, or project invalidates it.
5. Call `commit_storyboard(..., draft_preflight_id=...)` only with that exact full-scope, commit-ready certificate. Never reconstruct, guess, or reuse a certificate from another draft.
6. Send the immutable production handoff to the separate `independent_audit` authority and require the exact clean independent certificate before archive/sign-off.

The immutable first-draft quality target is: overall correctness at least `98%`, direct usability at least `98%`, every episode at least `96%`, and new local issue rate at most `2%`; hard errors, major issues, known-issue recurrence, and systemic duplicate patterns must each equal `0`. These thresholds grade the authored first draft and expose whether prevention worked; they do not waive a validator. A full preflight is commit-ready only when no issue remains. Final independent audit remains `100%` across all eight layers with blocking, residual, and introduced issues all `0`.

Keep responsibilities separate: production authors and repairs the canonical before commit; deterministic code projects outputs, enforces gates, binds hashes/certificates, and records every failed draft; independent audit reads the committed immutable artifact and searches for omissions without generating or repairing content. Audit is a second defense against missed semantic defects, not the primary cleaner for known template, schema, continuity, timing, or projection errors.

## Re-audit through fresh successor evidence

An explicit independent re-audit never reopens or overwrites a consumed production handoff. The server must mint one fresh, single-use successor handoff for the next continuous round, bound to the immutable root/parent lineage, original audit authority root and audit cycle, the root's active signed epoch, complete fingerprints, and distinct route/event traces. A failed result may issue one immutable repair package and `audit_to_repair` route only when every blocking issue resolves to at least one canonical shot; explicit `shot_ids` are authoritative and any location expression must resolve to the same target set. Only the latest completed failed lineage's package may authorize repair; every older package is superseded.

Every modern completed certificate binds the exact durable ledger event with `event_sha256`. Each successor additionally seals its immediate parent's certificate HMAC and event SHA inside the signed lineage, and storage rechecks that bridge under the project lock. A legacy certificate that lacks `event_sha256` is never current replay or sign-off authority: it may only be used as the historical parent of an explicit forced re-audit, after exact report/event validation, and becomes acceptable history only after the new successor seals that bridge and completes with a modern certificate.

The lifecycle Schema must fail closed on any partial successor marker: root/parent IDs, sequence, route/event/production trace fields, parent certificate/event bridge, original audit authority/session, server-minted flag and lineage SHA are one required structural authority set. Runtime and storage additionally require the route and event trace identities to be distinct and globally fresh. A successor preserves its root route and source role (`production_to_audit/production`, `repair_to_audit/repair`, or `audit_intake/external_input`) while remaining addressed to `independent_audit`; it must never coerce a repair or external-intake lineage into production authority. Its `prepared`, `consuming` and `consumed` claim shapes must match the signed storage state exactly; a consumed successor's current/latest certificate pointers and top-level/nested consumption-claim snapshots must agree under both runtime and storage validation. Runtime full-regression certificates are independent signed certificate records, never embedded Hub projections. Certificates for one runtime key use a project-lock monotonic signed `certificate_sequence`; same-second PASS/FAIL order never depends on filenames, and an ambiguous legacy same-second head fails closed. A legacy Hub duplicate must require control-state migration; migration removes only that retired projection and preserves the signed certificate records unchanged. Every nonterminal project migration journal (`prepared`, `replaying`, or `rolling_back`) globally fences ordinary writes; only the same migration ID may continue or roll back under a scoped permit. Execute caller CAS must equal the signed `from_version`, rollback caller CAS the signed `target_version`, and an explicit ID never falls back to another journal. A signed WAL and a current PASS runtime certificate rechecked inside the project lock are required before any after-image write; rolled-back re-execute first persists signed `replaying` intent. Prepared/replaying work may be cancelled by exact CAS into terminal `rolled_back`, while exact re-execute of `rolled_back` additionally requires the same unique key, certificate/rules/validator, before CAS, after images and changed paths. Otherwise preserve terminal history and use a new authorized migration work order. Every rejection is byte-level non-mutating.

A failure cannot become PASS by re-auditing the same bytes. The legal closure is failed audit → latest signed repair package → new repaired artifact and handoff with fresh file/source_version/artifact/canonical/trace bindings → return to the original audit authority. Repair accepts only `production_to_audit`, `repair_to_audit`, or `audit_intake` as its exact source route. External intake has no producer telemetry: its root trace is the independent-audit trace that created the intake, and audit completion supplies the verified canonical CAS used by full or patch repair. Crash recovery may resume only the exact server-minted route with the same normalized semantic submission. Normalization lowercases only a valid 64-hex caller `semantic_audit.canonical_sha256`; every other semantic, evidence and identity field remains exact, so a case-only replay of that SHA returns the same signed receipt without minting a successor or adding an event, certificate or round. If the ledger event is durable but its certificate is missing, derive and sign only that deterministic certificate after every event, handoff, trace, cache, fingerprint, result, issue and summary binding matches. An existing certificate must also match the complete deterministic certificate content and authority bindings; a mismatched certificate remains invalid even if it was re-signed with a valid service HMAC, and the rejection changes no authority or projection. A different semantic submission or certificate-only state likewise fails closed with zero mutation. Replaying an already completed signed receipt is a read-only no-op and never burns another round. An `issued` repair package is recoverable only while no downstream repair evidence exists; `consuming` is never accepted as a completed replay; `consumed` is valid only with the exact repair work order, committed successor/base CAS, package-bound metadata, signed `repair_to_audit` handoff and Hub projection. A consumed package may be compensated back to `issued` only under its exact original repair claim token and claim generation, with the fixed base/successor transaction already `aborted`, both the signed handoff and Hub projection `rejected + aborted`, and no handoff claim, consumption snapshot, audit certificate or other independent-audit downstream reference. A prepared or committed base transaction, different claim, live/published handoff, or any audit downstream forbids compensation. Missing package or Hub state is otherwise recoverable only inside the same storage lock after revalidating zero downstream evidence, latest lineage, exact certificate HMAC and fenced single-finisher claim; a consumed package cannot be deleted and resurrected, and stale replay cannot repair projections or return obsolete authority.

## Enforce the single conflict order

Use this priority without reordering:

`安全合规 > 源文忠实 > 分集边界 > 台词容量 > 统计闭合 > 可见性 > 图生视频可执行性 > 节奏留存 > 美学细节`

Always enforce these resolved rules:

- Calculate dialogue at 2.8 Chinese characters/second plus pause budget. Treat 3.5 only as a non-boundary reference.
- Use 3–5 seconds = 0–1 blink and >5–10 seconds = 1–2 blinks; allow a 1–2 second no-blink hold for shock/recognition; never synchronize two people; omit blinking when eyes are invisible.
- Specify camera movement as type + subject + path/trigger + narrative effect. Allow one main movement per single shot.
- Describe important emotional shots with at least four visible actor-detail categories; never use an abstract emotion word as the full performance direction.
- Design core dialogue with sound strength, speech rhythm, breath, pause, emphasis, and ending tone; connect punctuation to delivery.
- Keep lighting sourced and continuous across a scene. Do not use bottom light for ordinary romance.
- Keep `光影`, `光源来源`, `光源方向`, and `色温倾向` semantically separate. Reject Kelvin/K values in `色温倾向`, alternative sources containing “或”, and vague temperature labels without a scene-specific warm/cool spatial relationship.
- Keep `source_lock.aspect_ratio=9:16`, and also write the exact phrase `9:16竖屏` once in every shot's `生成限制`, formal master and GPT projection so each shot is executable in a blank context. Continue to reject only the verbose retired phrases `9:16竖屏真人古装短剧截图质感` and `9:16竖屏真人古装短剧单帧`.
- Mark psychological audio as no mouth movement, no lip-sync, no subtitles, and excluded from role-dialogue counts.
- Fail any new R10-SPC-1 formal master that cannot execute its declared single-shot duration or whose `画面内容` is not exactly three standard stages, overlaps, leaves gaps, or does not close on the shot duration. Legacy optional/natural timing is read-only migration input. Do not apply the retired 4–15-second batch-packaging rule.
